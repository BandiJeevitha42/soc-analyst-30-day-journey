Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications and data against unauthorized access, damage, disruption and theft.

Imagine a company has 5,000 employees. Every day:
- Employees sign in to their computers.
- Servers store confidential information.
- Emails arrive from internal and external senders.
- Applications exchange data over networks.
- Attackers may attempt to steal passwords or install malware.

Someone needs to monitor the security signals, identify suspicious behavior and help respond to incidents. That's where the Security Operations Center (SOC) comes in.

Command	Why a SOC analyst cares
hostname	Identifies the computer involved in an alert
whoami	Helps establish which account is running a command
ipconfig	Helps identify network configuration
Get-Date	Helps interpret event timestamps
Get-Process	Helps inspect running programs
Get-WinEvent	Helps examine recorded system activity
